SQLAlchemy 2.0.56 is now available.

Release 2.0.56 contains a series of bugfixes backported from the 2.1.x series,
including several more asyncio fixes, among them a regression involving
cancellation during ``AsyncConnection.close()``, as well as an ORM fix where
criteria added via ``PropComparator.and_()`` in ``selectinload()`` or
``lazyload()`` could lose its parenthesization when the statement came from the
compiled cache, and a fix for "expanding" IN parameters whose generated names
could collide with other bound parameters in the same statement.

The detailed changelog for 2.0.56 is at `Changelog </changelog/CHANGES_2_0_56>`_.

SQLAlchemy 2.0.56 is available on the `Download Page </download.html>`_.
