SQLAlchemy 2.0.55 is now available.

Release 2.0.55 contains a series of bugfixes backported from the 2.1.x series.
Of particular note are many fixes involving correct handling of
asyncio task cancellation with active database connections as well as garbage
collection surrounding connections (particularly asyncio connections) and
cursors.

Task-cancellation and garbage collection issues that were previously difficult
or impossible to reproduce are now being reported by users at a dramatically
higher rate.  There are two factors at play in this change.  One is that while
Python asyncio programming is typically straightforward when dealing with IO
streams and ``await`` keywords, the presence of the ``task.cancel()`` and
``CancelledError`` constructs adds a deeply complex layer to asyncio
applications, turning every ``await`` into a potential source of an exception
that requires context-specific cleanup of all resources and proper
re-propagation when it occurs.  The other is that these issues were previously
very difficult to report as they are transient and often nearly impossible to
reproduce, and when they were reported we were often unable to reproduce them.
However, with the advent of large language models, users can now isolate report
these issues with accurate reproduction cases, allowing them to be fixed.

As a result of these new capabilities, SQLAlchemy's internal testing now makes
use of a test harness that exercises a ``task.cancel()`` scenario at every
``await`` along an entire chain of operations, ensuring that for each one,
database connections are not "leaked" whether or not they are marked as
invalid, and the original ``CancelledError`` is correctly propagated.
