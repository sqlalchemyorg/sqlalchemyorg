SQLAlchemy 2.1.7 is now available.

Release 2.1.7 fixes a series of regressions in the 2.1 series, mostly in the
ORM, including issues with ORM-native dataclass mappings, ``selectinload()``
against aliased many-to-many targets, and execution options applied within
SAVEPOINTs, as well as a pool regression where garbage collected connections
were not returned to the pool on Python 3.15.  The release also adds new
``SessionTransaction.connections`` and ``SessionTransaction.bound_connection()``
accessors, improves typing so that constructs such as ``Column(Integer)`` may
be assigned to a wider declared type such as ``Column[int | None]``, and
includes the fixes that were also released in 2.0.56.

The detailed changelog for 2.1.7 is at `Changelog </changelog/CHANGES_2_1_7>`_.

SQLAlchemy 2.1.7 is available on the `Download Page </download.html>`_.
