SQLAlchemy 2.1.9 is now available.

Release 2.1.9 fixes two regressions in the 2.1 series, one where ORM mapped
dataclasses using ``orm_insert_sentinel()`` or a ``Sequence`` or
``ColumnDefault`` within ``mapped_column()`` would fail to flush, and one
where a relationship against a correlated LATERAL subquery would lose its
correlation when joined eager loaded with DISTINCT or GROUP BY.  The release
also fixes the same LATERAL issue for the other loader strategies, improves the
performance of ``Result.mappings()`` when the Cython extensions are in use, and
includes the fixes that were also released in 2.0.58, several of which
address issues located using automated fuzz testing.

The detailed changelog for 2.1.9 is at `Changelog </changelog/CHANGES_2_1_9>`_.

SQLAlchemy 2.1.9 is available on the `Download Page </download.html>`_.
