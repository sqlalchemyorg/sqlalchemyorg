SQLAlchemy 2.0.58 is now available.

Release 2.0.58 contains a series of bugfixes backported from the 2.1.x series,
mostly in the ORM, where several loader options such as those nested within
``selectin_polymorphic()`` or ``subqueryload()`` could be skipped when the
statement came from the compiled cache, along with fixes for joined eager
loading of inheritance subclasses and for ``post_update`` relationships.  The
release also fixes cache key issues involving ``Select.correlate()`` and
``Select.params()``, as well as reflection of the PostgreSQL ``TSQUERY`` and
``REGCONFIG`` types.  Several of these issues were located using automated
fuzz testing.

The detailed changelog for 2.0.58 is at `Changelog </changelog/CHANGES_2_0_58>`_.

SQLAlchemy 2.0.58 is available on the `Download Page </download.html>`_.
